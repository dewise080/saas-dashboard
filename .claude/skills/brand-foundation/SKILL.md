# Brand Foundation

Do not draw first. Establish the soul of the brand first.

## Goal
Create a brand identity that is emotionally grounded, strategically clear, and difficult to confuse with generic AI/SaaS design.

## Phase 1 — Emotive Narrative
Write a short narrative covering:
1. The human moment: what frustration or ambition makes this brand matter?
2. The transformation: what becomes possible after the product works?
3. The ethos: what does the brand believe?
4. The personality: how should the brand feel in the room?
5. The north star: one sentence that guides visual decisions.

The narrative must be concrete. Reject empty adjectives like innovative, modern, powerful, premium unless you explain what they mean visually and emotionally.

## Phase 2 — Strategic Foundation
Define:
- audience
- problem
- promised transformation
- category
- positioning
- core metaphor
- 3 emotional target words
- 3 personality traits
- 3 things the brand must never feel like

## Phase 3 — Visual Direction
Develop 3–5 genuinely different visual territories. They must differ in structural logic, not merely color.

For each direction define:
- central idea
- visual metaphor
- typography character
- shape language
- color philosophy
- motion behavior
- what category convention it rejects
- why it belongs to this brand specifically

## Phase 4 — Diverge / Kill / Mutate
Use an evolutionary process:
1. Diverge: generate structurally different directions.
2. Kill: owner makes binary decisions; dead directions stay dead.
3. Mutate: push the surviving ideas further, adding intentional distinctive breaks.
4. Repeat until the brand feels ownable rather than averaged.

Never blend every liked element together. Blending tends to average the identity back toward generic design.

## Brand Output
Create/update `.agents/brand.md` with:
- brand promise
- emotional target
- positioning
- core metaphor
- visual direction
- anti-reference list
- logo brief

## Runs It All specific guidance
If the brand is Runs It All, work from the product truth:
- many customer-facing operations become one coordinated system
- the owner gains control, calm, leverage, and continuity
- the product can expand beyond WhatsApp into calls, social channels, web, bookings, payments, reviews, broadcasts, feedback, analytics, and business-gap detection
- the identity must therefore feel broader than messaging, chatbots, or booking software

Potential conceptual territories include: orchestration, convergence, continuity, command, many-to-one, autonomous operation, and calm control.

Avoid generic AI symbolism: robot heads, sparkles, random nodes, generic infinity loops, chat bubbles, gears, magic wands, and decorative gradients without meaning.
